- **Polpetto in the startup header.** The TUI now opens a fresh session with a header — tentacle and its version, the model and folder, and the `/help` hint — which replaces the old `Tentacle ready.` line; beside it sits a small pixel mascot in one of nine colours picked at random each start. It is hidden when colours are off (`NO_COLOR`, not a terminal), on a 16-colour terminal, or when the window is too narrow for the text beside it.
