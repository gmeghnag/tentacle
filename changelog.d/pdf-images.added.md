- **Tool results can carry images to the model.** A tool result's images reach a vision model: inside the `tool_result` on Anthropic, and in one wire-only message right after the tool results on an OpenAI-compatible endpoint with `model.images: true`. The newest 20 tool images per request are sent, a budget separate from your own 5. Every image that is not sent leaves a visible line saying why (older than the newest 20, no vision, unavailable) — never a silent drop.
