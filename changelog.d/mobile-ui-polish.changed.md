- **iPhone app: a new look, starting with Computers and Pair.** One set of colours, type sizes and spacing, taken from the approved mockups, in light and dark mode. Computers has a large title with round Settings and "+" buttons, each computer gets a bold header with a status dot (Online, Offline, or what needs your attention) above a rounded card with its project, and a "Pair a computer" card sits at the bottom. Pair again and Remove now live in a menu: long-press the project or tap "…". A computer that needs a new pairing (including a revoked one) shows "Pair again" right in its card. Pairing shows the camera full screen with a viewfinder, and the review, the code check (the 6 digits in two large boxes) and the result appear in a bottom sheet. Design: `docs/feat/mobile-ui-polish.md`.
