- **Mobile: native iOS sheets with three detents.** The five session sheets (permission, outbox decision, commands, tools, session info) are now native `formSheet` routes instead of React Native modals: each opens at the middle detent, drags between short / medium / large, dims the conversation behind it, expands to large on a scroll, and keeps the layout you left when it re-opens. A permission ask in progress can no longer be dismissed by a swipe or a backdrop tap — "Not now" withdraws it explicitly — and the lock now drains every sheet before the app leaves the screen, so nothing of a session is left in the app switcher; the same sheets come back after unlocking, with the ask still pending.
