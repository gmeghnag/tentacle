- **A DSML re-ask no longer eats the step budget, and `/compact` refuses a summary cut at the output limit.** When a model leaked a tool call as DSML text, the one corrective re-ask was meant to be free but actually cost two steps — with a small cap (sub-agents) a single leak could spend the whole budget and jump straight to the forced step-limit report; it now costs nothing, like the re-ask after a reply cut at the output limit. Compaction used to accept a summary the provider had cut off (`finish_reason: length`, or a stream that ended with no stop reason) and replace the whole conversation with it, silently losing the end of the brief; it now fails with a clear error and leaves the conversation untouched, so you can retry.
