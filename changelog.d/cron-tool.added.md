- **The agent can schedule its own wake-ups.** Ask it to "check the build every 10 minutes" or "remind me at 17:00": it registers a scheduled wake (recurring or one-shot) that starts a turn with a short prompt when it is due. You are asked before each one is created, and the host's `schedules.*` config sets the limits (by default 10 per session, at least 1 minute apart, recurring ones expire after 7 days). `/schedule` (alias `/schedules`) is a panel listing each wake with its quoted prompt, next fire and fire count; in the terminal the pill carries `2 sched` and the background dropdown their rows (`ctrl+x` twice deletes one), a fired turn is styled `◷ scheduled · sc-1 every 10m · fire 5` (falling back to the plain `[scheduled …]` prefix after a reload), quitting a session with armed wakes asks first, and a wake that leaves the table says so in the transcript (a deliberate delete says that instead, and an expired wake names its expiry). Session info on the iPhone lists them too. On `serve` they survive a daemon restart, in a local session they last until you quit. Design: `docs/feat/cron-tool.md`.
