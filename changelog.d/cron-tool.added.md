- **The agent can schedule its own wake-ups.** Ask it to "check the build every 10 minutes" or "remind me at 17:00": it registers a scheduled wake (recurring or one-shot) that starts a turn with a short prompt when it is due. You are asked before each one is created, and the host's `schedules.*` config sets the limits (by default 10 per session, at least 1 minute apart, recurring ones expire after 7 days). `/schedule` (and Session info on the iPhone) lists them and deletes one; on `serve` they survive a daemon restart, in a local session they last until you quit. Design: `docs/feat/cron-tool.md`.
