- **Diagrams get a lint of their own.** The pure half of the `diagram` tool lands first: a Mermaid source is normalised (a Markdown fence, a config front-matter, `%%{init}%%` and hard-coded styling are dropped with a note the model reads back instead of a retry), refused with a fix named when it is unrecognisable (an unknown or misspelt type, a numbers chart that belongs to `chart`, a source over its caps), and read into a small structural model of the flowchart and sequence subset — the same reading that will feed the tool's result line and the phone's VoiceOver summary. Mermaid's own parse errors, in both of its shapes, become one sentence with the model's line and the fix. Nothing is drawn yet: this slice is the shared pure layer (`packages/core/src/diagram/`).
