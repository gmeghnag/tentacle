Agent workflows (docs/feat/agent-workflows.md D5): a permission_request whose tool is workflow_approve (the approval of a workflow's content hash, and a workflow's approve step such as a merge) must NEVER be auto-accepted. An older client's auto-accept would answer it allow-once, approving a merge with no human, so a mixed pair must fail at initialize instead. Additive in the same task: the session/workflow and workflow/list methods, the display update workflows, the snapshot field workflows, and three non-critical log entry types workflow_start, workflow_step and workflow_end.
